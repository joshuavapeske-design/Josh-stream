Quick Troubleshooting

Video won't play? Try clicking a different server button (e.g., Server 2 or Server 3) directly below the player.

Lag or buffering? Ensure hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser settings for smooth 60fps HD video decoding.

Audio out of sync? Pausing the video for 2 seconds and resuming usually re-synchronizes the audio stream buffer.
