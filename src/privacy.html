Privacy Policy & Terms

Transparent, straightforward guidelines on how LUPERTA handles your data, ensures safe browsing, and maintains our indexer platform.

Effective: January 1, 2026 • Version: 3.2.0 • Compliance: GDPR / CCPA / DMCA

🛡️ Privacy at a Glance

LUPERTA is built with a privacy-first mindset. We do not require account creation, do not ask for credit card numbers, and do not track your personal identity across the web. Your favorites, watch history, and theme settings are saved locally inside your own browser via HTML5 localStorage.

01. Information We Collect

Unlike traditional streaming platforms, LUPERTA requires no mandatory account registration. We collect data solely to maintain platform stability and deliver streaming media embeds:

  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized server logs including browser user agent, preferred language, access timestamps, and requested movie/show IDs to balance CDN workloads.
  • Local Device Preferences: Your theme toggle (dark/light), episode progress bookmarks, and custom watchlist entries are stored strictly inside your browser's localStorage. This data never touches our backend servers.
  • Support Communications: If you voluntarily reach out via our Contact page, we collect your submitted email address and message contents solely to resolve your technical query.

02. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We minimize tracking to the absolute lowest technical necessity:

  • We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies or commercial ad trackers.
  • Temporary session identifiers may be used strictly for DDoS mitigation (e.g. Cloudflare or hosting reverse proxies).
  • Embedded third-party video players (e.g., streaming mirrors and YouTube trailer embeds) may load their own cookies according to their respective privacy disclosures. We recommend using privacy-focused browser configurations if you wish to block third-party cookies.

03. Third-Party Service Providers & APIs

LUPERTA interacts with reputable third-party services to render media information and streaming streams:

The Movie Database (TMDB)

Provides movie metadata, posters, casts, and synopses. Queries are handled directly via client-side API requests adhering to TMDB API guidelines.

YouTube Embeds

Official trailers are loaded via YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain (youtube-nocookie.com) where supported.

Multi-Host Streaming Mirrors

Third-party player embeds operate independently. When you click play, you communicate directly with the respective mirror server.

Content Delivery Networks

Assets and static bundles are distributed globally via high-speed CDNs to minimize latency and ensure 99.9% uptime.

04. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

Whether you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to comprehensive data privacy rights under GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request disclosure of any personal data we hold associated with your IP address or support communications.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You can wipe all local preferences instantly by clearing your browser cache and localStorage. For server log records or support tickets, email our privacy team.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not sell personal information, and we never restrict streaming features based on privacy choices.
  • Do Not Track (DNT) Signals: LUPERTA honors universal opt-out preference signals and DNT headers.

05. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

LUPERTA is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). If we learn that a minor has provided us with personal information through support channels, we immediately purge such records from our systems.

06. Data Security & Inquiries

All network communication with LUPERTA is secured through modern TLS 1.3 encryption. For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal rights, please reach out via our dedicated form at Contact Us or email privacy@luperta.stream.
